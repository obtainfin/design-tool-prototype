FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know about working with a mortgage broker.

A mortgage broker acts as the intermediary between you and lenders. Instead of going directly to one bank, we search across our entire panel to find the most suitable loan product for your needs. We handle the research, paperwork, and negotiation — you just make the decision.
Our broking service is completely free to you. We receive a commission from the lender when your loan settles. We are legally required to act in your best interests regardless — this is enforced by ASIC's Best Interests Duty legislation. We'll always disclose our commission upfront.
Pre-approval can typically be obtained in 24–48 hours once we have all your documents. Full formal approval generally takes 3–7 business days depending on the lender. We'll keep you informed at every stage and chase the lender proactively on your behalf.
We have access to 40+ lenders including all major banks (ANZ, CBA, NAB, Westpac), mid-tier banks (ING, Macquarie, Suncorp, St George), and specialist/non-bank lenders. This breadth means we can almost always find a competitive option even for complex situations.
Yes. We have access to specialist lenders who cater to self-employed borrowers (including those with alt-doc options) and borrowers with impaired credit history. Every situation is different — book a free consultation and we'll assess what's achievable for you.
Typically you'll need: 2 recent payslips (or last 2 years tax returns if self-employed), last 3 months bank statements, photo ID, and details of any existing debts or assets. We'll give you a personalised checklist after your initial consultation so nothing is missed.
